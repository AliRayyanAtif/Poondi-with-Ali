I.

What is Poondi

Poondi is not complicated. You're out in the world — campus, market, wedding, wherever — and you spot him. A specimen. You take in the whole presentation, and you assign a number, one through ten. That's it. That's the sport.

It's less about being harsh and more about paying attention. Most of the world walks past God's finer work without ever looking up. Poondi is the practice of looking up.
